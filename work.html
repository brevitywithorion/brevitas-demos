Studio process

Demo this week. Live after you approve.

No case-study theater. The work is the template with your name on it, a listing that matches, and a written recap of scope. Price is a one-time setup — $2,500–$6,500, or $1,200 charter for the first five closed Lawrence trades — spoken after a look at your Maps page, not on the sidewalk. Domain and two years hosting included. You own the files.

  1. Call, email, or note

    Call or email first. Two minutes on your Google listing versus a competitor with photos and hours. No cold visits. Visit only if you ask.

  2. Industry demo

    We duplicate the matching template, drop in your name, address, and phone, and show it on a laptop or send a link the same day when we can.

  3. Listing audit

    Pin, categories, photo recency, Q&A, whether the listed number actually rings. Screenshot before we change anything.

  4. Written close

    Scope is the site plus listing hygiene. Not included: ads, print, or invented service areas. Verbal yes plus email is enough to start. Invoice is one-time.

  5. Onboard

    Legal name, NAP, hours, eight or more photos, and either GBP admin access or a scheduled screenshare. Then JSON-LD, map embed, and tel links. Domain registered in your name.

  6. Fourteen-day check

    You post one photo and ask three customers for reviews in person. We look at the pin, holiday hours, and whether the first replies sound like a person. We never buy reviews.

Questions

What we will not promise.

Do you guarantee Maps rank or AI Overviews?

No. We do NAP match, a real primary category, photos, and a fast page. That is the work. Rankings and Google AI Overviews are not for sale here.

Is there a monthly fee?

No retainer. Launch includes the domain and two years live. After 24 months you renew the name at registrar cost. You can take a zip anytime.
