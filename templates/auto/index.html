Questions

FAQ

Do you take walk-ins?

Oil changes often. Diagnostics need a slot — call the demo line and we will show how a live shop would book you.

How is pricing set?

Labor is posted by the hour. Most brake jobs for a sedan fall in a mid-hundreds range; we quote before the wrench turns.

Tow or after-hours?

We do not run a wrecker. If you are stranded, we can recommend a towing partner. Overnight drop keys are a live-shop option.

Service area?

Lawrence, Eudora, Baldwin City. We work on cars that live here, not fleet contracts out of state.
