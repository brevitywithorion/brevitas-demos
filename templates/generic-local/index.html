About

Meet Quinn Adler

Founder Quinn Adler sketched Riverfront Co. as a sample third place: not a cafe, not a coworking chain. The invented story is a long room, good light, and a staff of three who know regulars by name.

Service area: Lawrence, Eudora, and Baldwin City. This page is a Brevitas sample — swap the story for yours at launch.

FAQ

Can I drop in?

Weekdays usually. Weekends are workshop-first — call the demo line.

What does it cost?

Hourly studio time is a modest two-digit rate. Memberships are quoted monthly. Workshops listed on the chalkboard.

After-hours access?

Members with a fob in a live shop. This demo does not issue keys.

Service area?

Lawrence first, then Eudora and Baldwin City makers who need a room.
