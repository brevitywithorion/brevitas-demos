Questions

FAQ

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes most weeknights. Friday and Saturday we recommend a call — this is a sample policy, not a live reservation desk.

What does dinner cost?

Most mains land between $18 and $32. Brunch plates are $12–$18. We print prices on the board, not in ads.

Is there an emergency / late seating policy?

The kitchen closes at the posted hour. If you are running late, call the demo line; we will not hold a table past 15 minutes on a full night.

Do you serve Lawrence, Eudora, and Baldwin?

Yes — dine-in downtown, catering across Douglas County.
