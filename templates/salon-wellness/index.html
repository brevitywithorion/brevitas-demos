FAQ

How do I book?

A live salon would embed a scheduler. This demo uses click-to-call so you can feel the mobile path.

What do services cost?

Cuts typically $45–$85, color starts in the $90s depending on length and correction. We quote before we mix.

Cancellation policy?

24 hours, or the card on file is a sample no-show fee. Emergency same-day is at the stylist’s discretion.

Do you serve only Lawrence?

Clients drive from Eudora and Baldwin. Parking is on the sample lot behind 415 Example Court.
