FAQ

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes for no-heat, no-cool in extreme weather, and active leaks. After-hours is a call-out fee plus parts — stated on the phone before we roll.

What does a typical call cost?

Diagnostic visits are a flat trip charge in the $89–$129 range (sample). Repairs are quoted before we start; replacements get a written option sheet.

Where do you work?

Lawrence, Eudora, Baldwin City, and Lecompton. We do not fake a Kansas City radius.
