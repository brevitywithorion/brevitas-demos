Questions

FAQ

Do you take walk-ins in April?

No. Sample policy: scheduled only. Emergency filings get a same-week slot if we have one.

How do fees work?

Monthly bookkeeping is a fixed range by transaction volume. Individual tax often lands in the mid-hundreds; business returns are scoped in writing.

Who do you serve?

Lawrence, Eudora, Baldwin City owners, plus a few remote Kansas clients. We are not a national mill.

Are you hiring this demo?

No — fictional firm. Real engagement would start with a consult call.
