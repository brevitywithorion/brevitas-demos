Questions

Plain answers.

What does Brevitas actually build?

A one-page website with your name, hours, photos, map, and click-to-call, plus Google Business Profile hygiene so the listing matches the page. We do not sell ads, twelve-page brochures, or ranking retainers.

How much does a Brevitas site cost?

One-time setup is $2,500–$6,500 depending on photos and how messy the listing is. The first five closed Lawrence trades pay a charter price of $1,200. Domain name and two years of hosting are included. There is no monthly retainer.

Do I own the domain, listing, and files?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name. After 24 months you renew it at registrar cost. You own the Google Business Profile and a zip of the files anytime. If you already have a domain, you keep the registrar; we only send A or CNAME records.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or AI Overviews?

No. We match name, address, and phone, set a real category, and put a fast page on the web. That is hygiene, not a ranking promise and not a claim about Google AI Overviews.

Who is this for?

Owner-operated trades in Lawrence and nearby towns—HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing first—plus shops that answer the phone. We are not a fit for franchise CMS, a site you already like, or anyone who wants fake reviews or a guaranteed #1.

How long until it is live?

We can show an industry demo the same week you share hours and photos. We go live after you approve NAP and pictures. You preview before anything public.
